## Meta-observations

- **Most unanimous axis:** PC4 (Drive). Every rater used "drive," "intensity," or "motivated." More unanimous than valence.
- **Biggest Opus/Sonnet split:** PC2. Opuses all said "*hostile* arousal." Sonnets mostly said "arousal/activation." Opuses noticed the dominance coloring; Sonnets gave the textbook answer.
- **Shortest response:** The Poet (Sonnet 10). Ten labels, no explanations. Called PC7 "Grief."
- **PC7 consensus failure:** Opuses leaned "resignation." Sonnets scattered across "existential weight," "grief," "turbulence," "disorientation," and "emotional incoherence." Post-hoc candidate label: **Purpose(?)** — states oriented around internal purpose vs states oriented around social reaction.
